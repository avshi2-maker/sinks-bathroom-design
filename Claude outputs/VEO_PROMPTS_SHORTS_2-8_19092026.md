# Marble Art — Veo prompts, Shorts 2–8 (19/09/2026)

**Rules that made Week 1 pass:** use the **Video/Veo** tool (or Google Flow), 9:16 vertical, **no background music** (SFX/ambience only), don't mention "the attached image" in the text, keep it one paragraph. Add Hebrew on-screen captions in **CapCut**, not in Veo. ~8s native → Extend for 10s.

**Honest split:** AI is great at macro stone / water / light (weeks 3,4). It's weak at human hands doing skilled work (carving, install). Those are marked **📱 FILM REAL** — a 15-sec phone clip beats a fake AI one. AI prompts still given as fallback.
